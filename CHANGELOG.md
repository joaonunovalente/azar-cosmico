# Changelog

All notable changes to Azar Cósmico are documented here.

## Version 2 - 2026-10-17

### New Azar Cósmico site

- O Azar Cósmico now features a more modern design while preserving the original content.
- The website is now fully responsive, with support for light and dark themes. It also presents a new logo and favicon, along with accessibility improvements.
- The newsletter functionality and the Contact page were removed. The Reviews page was replaced by a carousel on the homepage.
- The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Changelog pages were added.

## Version 1 - 2020-10-21

### Launch of Azar Cósmico

- Azar Cósmico launches with pages for episodes, blog, and videos.
- Episodes can be played directly on the site and visitors can subscribe to the newsletter.
